01 / ABOUT

I turn complex requirements into clear, working systems.

I’m a full-stack developer based in Tehran, focused on building products that are useful, maintainable, and ready to grow.

My work spans polished React interfaces, modular backend services, database architecture, automation, and deployment. I enjoy understanding the entire system, then choosing the simplest architecture that can do the job well.

Beyond product work, I explore framework internals, algorithms, AI embeddings, and developer tooling. That curiosity led me to create Homa.js, a zero-dependency TypeScript web framework for Node.js.
